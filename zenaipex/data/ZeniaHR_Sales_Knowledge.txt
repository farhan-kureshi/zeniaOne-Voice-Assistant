# ZeniaHR Product and Sales Knowledge

## 1. Product Overview
ZeniaHR is a comprehensive Human Resources Management System (HRMS) designed to streamline and automate core HR operations. It centralizes employee data, attendance tracking, leave management, and payroll processing into a single, secure platform. ZeniaHR empowers HR teams to reduce administrative overhead and improve organizational efficiency.

## 2. Core Features
- **Centralized Employee Database**: Maintain secure, digital records for all employees including personal details, job history, and documents.
- **Automated Workflows**: Streamline onboarding, offboarding, and standard HR requests.
- **Self-Service Portal**: Employees can access their own profiles, request leaves, and download payslips without HR intervention.
- **Reporting & Analytics**: Generate comprehensive reports on attendance trends, headcount, and payroll distribution.

## 3. HR Modules
- **Attendance Management**: Real-time tracking of employee check-ins and check-outs, shift scheduling, and integration with biometric devices.
- **Leave Management**: Configurable leave policies, multi-level approval workflows, and automated accrual calculations.
- **Payroll Processing**: Automated salary calculation, tax deductions, compliance handling, and direct deposit integration.
- **Performance Management**: Goal setting, continuous feedback, and standardized appraisal cycles.
- **Recruitment (ATS)**: Applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and offer letter generation.

## 4. Admin Benefits
- Reduces manual data entry errors.
- Ensures regulatory compliance with built-in labor law configurations.
- Provides granular role-based access control (RBAC) to protect sensitive compensation data.
- Cloud-based architecture ensures high availability and remote accessibility.
