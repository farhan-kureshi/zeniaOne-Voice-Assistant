The money questions

How does volume pricing actually work?

Your whole headcount sits at one rate, decided by your team size. At 101 users everyone is ₹20, so you pay 101 × ₹20 = ₹2,020 a month, not 100 at ₹25 plus 1 at ₹20. There are no stacked slabs.

What counts as a user?

Every person on the system, including admins. On the free plan the 2 admins are part of your 50.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It runs indefinitely for up to 50 users and 2 admins. You only move to a paid plan when you outgrow it or need geotagged attendance, the full report set, more admins, or phone support.

What does "per user per month, billed quarterly" mean?

The rate is quoted per user per month. On quarterly billing you are invoiced every three months for three months at that rate. On yearly billing you are invoiced once for twelve months, at a per-user rate that is 20% lower.

What happens when we grow past 100 or 500?

Your whole team moves to the new band's rate. Growing never increases your per-user price, it lowers it.

We are well past 500. Is ₹15 the final rate?

₹15 quarterly / ₹12 yearly is published so you can budget without booking a call. If your scale or setup needs custom terms, we will work them out with you.

FAQ
Payroll software questions, answered

Which is the best payroll software for a manpower or staffing company in India?
ZeniaHR is built specifically for manpower and staffing firms: it runs payroll across multiple sites and clients from one muster, auto-computes PF, ESI, PT and LWF, and generates the statutory registers labour inspectors ask for. It is free for your first 50 workers.

Does ZeniaHR calculate PF, ESI, PT and LWF automatically?
Yes. Every salary run auto-computes employee and employer PF, ESI, professional tax by state, and labour welfare fund, and produces the ECR and challan-ready outputs so filing is a copy-paste, not a recalculation.

Is ZeniaHR cloud based?
Yes, ZeniaHR is cloud based, so supervisors mark attendance from any site on mobile and payroll is processed centrally. An on-premise, self-hosted deployment is available on request for firms that must keep data on their own infrastructure.

How does attendance become payroll?
Attendance captured at each site (mobile muster, biometric or geo-tagged punch) flows straight into the salary run. Overtime, shift rates, leave and advances are applied automatically, so a month that used to take days closes in minutes.

How much does ZeniaHR payroll cost?
It is free for up to 50 workers. Paid plans start at ₹25 per worker per month and fall to ₹12 at scale on yearly billing. See the

Your CTC is the total cost to the company, so it includes money you never see in cash, like the employer's PF and gratuity. Take-home salary is what is left after those and your own deductions.

Frequently asked questions

How is in-hand salary calculated from CTC?
In-hand salary is CTC minus employer PF and gratuity (which gives gross salary), minus your own PF contribution, professional tax and income tax. The result divided by 12 is your monthly take-home.

What is the difference between CTC and take-home salary?
CTC is the total cost to the company, including the employer's PF and gratuity. Take-home is what actually reaches your bank after those employer costs, your PF, professional tax and income tax are removed.

Which tax regime does this use?
The new tax regime for FY 2025-26, with a ₹75,000 standard deduction and full rebate on taxable income up to ₹12,00,000. It is an estimate; your actual tax depends on your regime choice and other income.

Is professional tax the same in every state?
No. Professional tax is a state levy and varies. Many states charge up to ₹2,500 a year and some do not levy it at all. Change the amount in the calculator to match your state.
