About ZeniaPex
We build the software
Indian businesses run on
ZeniaPex is a product-and-engineering company with a simple belief: good software should quietly do the heavy lifting so your team can focus on what matters.
We started ZeniaPex to close a gap we kept seeing: businesses had ideas, but got either off-the-shelf tools that didn't fit, or agencies that handed over a deck and disappeared.
So we built a team that can take an idea all the way. Web and mobile, AI and data, cloud and design, testing and support. And we proved it on ourselves, shipping our own products: ZeniaHR, Zenia Fleets, Tender Guruji and Zenia Chat.
That's the whole promise, and the reason for our name on the door: you think it, we code it. Grounded in one market we understand deeply, India.
