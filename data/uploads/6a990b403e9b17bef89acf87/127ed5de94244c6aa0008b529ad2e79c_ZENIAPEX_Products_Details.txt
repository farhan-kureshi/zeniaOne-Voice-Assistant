## SECTION 1: ABOUT ZENIAPEX (MAIN / PARENT COMPANY)

Company Name: ZeniaPex
Type: Technology Company (Parent / Holding Company)
Industry: Artificial Intelligence, SaaS, Enterprise Software, HR Technology

ZeniaPex is the main parent technology company that builds and manages a portfolio of software products and platforms. Each product under ZeniaPex is designed to solve a specific business problem using modern technology, AI, and cloud infrastructure. ZeniaPex is the umbrella brand under which all products and projects are developed and maintained.

The company focuses on:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) powered solutions
- Voice and telephony automation
- Enterprise business management software
- Multilingual Indian language technology
- SaaS (Software as a Service) platforms

ZeniaPex operates in the Indian market with strong support for Indian languages including Hindi, Hinglish, Gujarati, and English. The company is committed to building enterprise-grade, multi-tenant, secure, and scalable software.

## SECTION 2: ZENIAPEX PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

ZeniaPex has the following products and projects under its umbrella:

### PRODUCT 1: ZeniaOne (AI Voice Agent & Telephony Platform)

Product Name: ZeniaOne
Type: Primary AI Platform / SaaS Product
Parent Company: ZeniaPex
Category: Artificial Intelligence, Voice Technology, Telephony, Customer Support Automation

What is ZeniaOne?
ZeniaOne is ZeniaPex's flagship AI-powered Voice Agent and Telephony SaaS platform. It enables businesses to deploy autonomous AI agents that can hold natural, human-like voice conversations with customers. ZeniaOne is currently under active development and new features are being added regularly.

Key Features of ZeniaOne:
- Autonomous AI Voice Agents capable of natural human-like voice conversations
- 24/7 customer support automation without human intervention
- Instant appointment bookings and scheduling via voice
- Payment follow-ups and reminders through automated calls
- Real-time barge-in (natural interruption handling) with sub-second latency
- End-to-end multi-tenant isolation (multiple companies can use the platform independently)
- Native Indian multilingual support: English, Hindi, Hinglish, and Gujarati
- WebSocket-based real-time voice streaming
- Intelligent intent detection and RAG for accurate knowledge-based answers
- Browser-based voice interface with push-to-talk and hands-free modes
- Smart phonetic correction for Indian language voice input
- AI agent creation and management via admin panel
- Knowledge base document upload and RAG indexing

ZeniaOne Target Customers:
- Businesses (retail, service companies, clinics, hospitals)
- Companies needing 24/7 customer support
- Organizations wanting to automate phone calls and appointment systems
- Companies requiring Indian multilingual voice support

Current Status: ZeniaOne is under active development. Features are being added progressively.

### PRODUCT 2: ZeniaHR (Enterprise HRMS & Payroll Platform)

Product Name: ZeniaHR
Type: Enterprise Software / HRMS Product
Parent Company: ZeniaPex
Category: Human Resources Management System (HRMS), Payroll, Attendance, Staffing

What is ZeniaHR?
ZeniaHR is ZeniaPex's enterprise-grade Human Resource Management System (HRMS) and payroll platform. It is designed for staffing companies, enterprises, and organizations that manage multiple employees, branches, and clients. ZeniaHR handles the complete employee lifecycle from hiring to payroll processing.

Key Features of ZeniaHR:
- Multi-company (multi-tenant) architecture: keeps separate companies, branches, and staff data fully isolated
- Complete employee lifecycle management: from hiring and onboarding to deployment at client sites
- Attendance tracking and management (daily, monthly, shift-based)
- Payroll management with PF (Provident Fund) and ESIC compliance for Indian laws
- Client billing support for staffing companies (bill clients for deployed staff)
- White-labelling capabilities: use your own brand name and domain
- Built-in security features including CAPTCHA, account lockout, and encrypted passwords
- Role-based access control for HR managers, admins, and staff
- Salary slip generation and payroll reports
- Leave management and holiday calendar

ZeniaHR Target Customers:
- Staffing and manpower supply companies
- Enterprises with large workforces
- Companies with multiple branches or locations
- Organizations needing PF/ESIC compliance automation
